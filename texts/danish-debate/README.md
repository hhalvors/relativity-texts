# The Danish relativity debate, 1912–1917

These editions are MIRRORED from `~/danish-texts/texts/kroman/` by
`make mirror` (see `MIRROR.tsv` at the repo root). danish-texts is where they
are made, checked and corrected; the copies here are overwritten on every
mirror. Do not edit them here.

The record of each edition (source, page map, conventions, rights notes) is in
the header of its `transcription.tex`. H. M. Hansen's texts carry a header
saying they are not to be published before 1 January 2027 (Danish copyright,
life + 70; Hansen died 1956). That rule holds in danish-texts. In this repo
Hans decided on 2026-10-21 to publish Hansen's 1916 reply now: it was published
in 1916 and is in the public domain in the United States, as for Laue and Born.
